     "Come Parepa to the garden," said Daddy.  "I know you will love
it."

     "I'm crazy about gardens.  Frances remembers that, I'm sure,"
she said.  They wondered about for some time Daddy explaining the rare
specimens and origin and how they came into possession of them.  It
was all very interesting to Parepa.

     Finally they came to a most welcome garden seat in the shade of
some tall desert shrubs.  "Let's set here and talk," he suggested.
"There is much I must tell you though I hate to come to the subject.
I know it is best to get what business we have  to transact done as
quickly as possible."

     They sat down and he turned to her saying, "The Doctor's don't en-⟦hyphen⟧
courage me in thinking Frances can live more than a month or six weeks.
She may go at any minute.  It is her greatest desire to see you and
your family well looked after, before that time comes.  I think the
transfers should be made tomorrow.  All that is necessary is  for
Frances to transfer her holdings in the many banks to you.  I'll see
that they all go through and that everything is settled before you go
home," he said almost in one breath.

     Parepa sat silent.

     Finally he asked, "Do you understand, Parepa?"
