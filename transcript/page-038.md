                                                                    36
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "No, we have timed this thing so an answer can't possibly get
back to us before I leave.  I told them you'd stay here until I see
them on my trip and talk it over then."
     "I won't leave anything unsaid that should be said in favor of
your staying, you can bet on that," and Uncle George chuckled her
under the chin.
     "Now Parepa you go practice and George and I will do the dishes",
said Aunt Kate.
     She obeyed.  As she was all concentration on her lesson she didn't
hear the front door open quietly and some one slip up behind her putting
both hands over her eyes and with a rousing kiss on her cheek say,
"Guess who."
     She knew it was Frances.  When he took her hands from over her
eyes he gave a ringing laugh that brought Mr. and Mrs. Slay from the
kitchen.
     Parepa was blushing a deep crimson.  After greetings were exchanged
Aunt Kate said, "I'm sorry Frances, but we have planned a definite time
for Parepa's piano practice and it must not be interfered with.  Won't
you come into the kitchen with us until she finishes?"
     "You bet.  I'm sorry I was so rude," he said to Parepa as he
passed her.
     When the practice was over they all sat on the porch and joked and
sang.  Finally Parepa ventured to tell the thing she had been wanting to
tell Frances all evening.
     "Did you know I've got a job?" she said timidly.
     "You?  A job?" he laughed and pretended to fall off his chair.
"What kind of a job did you get?"
