"What's it all about, Wifie?" asked her patient husband.
     "Oh, it seems as if someone who knew me when I went to the Bethany
Church in Peoria is trying to locate me again," she said as she turned
to answer the 'phone which was ringing frantically.  The following con-
versation took place:
     "This is Mrs Bradford?"
     "Peoria is calling you, hold the line," said the operator.
     Allie heard the coins tinkle into the 'phone box.  She hoped no
one was going to repeat the news.
     "Hello, Parepa.  I bet you will never guess who is talking to you."
     "No, I don't recognize your voice."
     "Well, it's an old friend of thirty years ago.  Do you remember
Frank Johnston of old Bethany picnic days?"
     "Why, yes, I do.  But what brings you here?" she asked laughingly.
     "Oh, wait till I tell you.  In the paper tonight I saw your name
in the headlines.  I remembered you well and set about to find out your
present name.  Minnie Bryden told me and I'm calling up to tell you
that I think you are in for some good luck.  I hope you will pardon me."
     "CERtainly, but what good luck do you refer too?  I am altogether
in the dark."
