     Parepa hurried down the five blocks of Hayward Street to the
river.  She was lonesome for the memories of the night she had spent
two years ago with Frances on the river.  She was a little chilly now
and throwing her long blue cape around her shoulders she crouched down
beside the big log on which she was sure she and Frances had sat in the
light of the moon and stars that night of mad love which Parepa held
dearest of all.
     It all came back to her now.  The boat ride, the moon, the songs
they sang, the love pledges, this log on which they sat when first she
saw the mysterious locket.  "Frances!  Oh, Frances!" she cried⟦strikeover: "i" overtyped on another letter⟧ in a
