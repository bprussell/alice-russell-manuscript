        Frances laughed outright at this young girl's frankness "And what
did your fine husband do, and look like in all these mystic moments?" he
asked.
        "That's the funny part of it, Frances, I can't remember of having
one.  Seems I was so happy just having all the beautiful things I wanted,
my husband was the least of my thoughts.  I had children though, the sweet-⟦hyphen⟧
est ones in the world, but really I can't remember a thing about my husband.
I can remember too, of how many, many gifts I gave to folks.  I was never
so happy in my dreams as when I was giving giving giving to someone and
doing all sorts of things to make folks happy.  Seemed as if the money
supply was never exhausted or at least I didn't worry about it as long
as I had plenty to give with.
