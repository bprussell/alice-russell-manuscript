                                                              158
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

planning for her son a life of goodness, helpfulness, and purity.
She seemed to realize from the first that it would be impossible for
us both to survive and she bravely reconciled herself to the fact.  Her
family for years had accumulated vast fortunes in turpentine.  It was
her wish that my identity be lost but the fortune kept in tact by my
guardian that I might when old enough use it where it would do humanity
the most good.  This was to be my mission in life.
     All of the mothers whose faces you have seen tonight were kind
and loving.  Their sexual weakness being their only fault.  It ruined
the lives and name of my family.  From the minute I was born every
wish of my mothers has been carried out.  My name which she gave me
is Frances LaVern Emmerson.  I have been raised a real fellow from the
start.  All the physical exercise for the proper development of a
strong well formed boy was carefully adhered to.  I grew to be an
excellent sportsman, skilled in every form of athletic fetes.  I have
won medals and many loving cups for swimming, tennis, bowling, vaulting,
running, riding, driving, rowing, jumping, and discus throwing.  I
love my music.  It is the most comforting accomplishment I have.  I can
always find pleasure in good books.  No boy ever grew to manhood more
carefully raised than I.  From the time I was old enough to understand
what a mother was I have missed her.
     She left letters for me to open on my birthday from the sixth
one up to the fourteenth.  Each one was written to interest a boy of
that age.  I was given the locket to wear on my fourteenth birthday.
It has been a part of me ever since.  The "secret" was in it.  Parepa,
dearest, have you guessed it?  Can you believe me?" and he whispered

the truth in her ear.  "Darling, I'm a Girl."
