                                                                  122

Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

Daddy Wheeler joined her and the many surprizes she found on the estate
kept her from getting tired.  They visited the stables.  Two of Frances's
fine horses which she brought from England with her were the pride of
the groom.  The dog kennels were cared for by an attendant who proudly
explained to Parepa the different breeds and their high points.  Every
day he exercised them.

     After they had dinner the chauffeur drove them through the city
of Tucson.  Truly she was in the heart of the "Garden of Allah".

     "You haven't been in one of the business houses since you came.
We'll have to take a shopping trip some day soon," Daddy offered.

     "I'd love to do that now that I have a checking account," she
laughed.  "I'd like to spend a whole week buying presents for the home
folks."

     "That's just what you shall do," he agreed.
