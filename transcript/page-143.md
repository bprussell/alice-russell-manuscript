        Reverently he placed this picture on the top of the others, then
folding the glass over them and snapping it tightly he turned to the
folded papers - messages from his mother.  Silently he scanned them.
The last one he held in his hand for a long time - apparently wondering
what he should do with it.  He slowly reached for a match, lit it and
held the paper over a metal tray as he lighted one corner of it and
deliberately watched it burn.  A knock at the door which he knew to be
Daddy Wheeler's caused him to drop the paper on the tray.
        After replacing the locket on his breast he opened the door to
his Daddy, his comforter, his worthy counselor, his father confessor.
