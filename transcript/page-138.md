                                                                  136
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

        Parepa went to church that evening as usual.  She sang a soloa and
a duet with Joseph.  She did not notice Frances slip quietly into a
back seat.  He sat as one fastened there  --  immovable as his eyes
followed the glances of the two young people.
        <u>That</u> is <u>real</u> love.  Love as it should be.  Love  --  his face is
shining with a fire that only a real mate's love can kindle.  The song
Parepa sang was so sweet and she so charming no wonder this normal
young chap had fallen in love with her.  She was his kind.  She should
be his wife  --  I'll not interfere.  He sat quietly in his seat, having
made his decision and listened to the sermon.  "Be not over anxious for
this world's goods" was the text.  What do all this world's good amount
to?  Nothing thought Frances.
        As soon as the benediction was pronounced Frances crept out of the
church and into the street.  He decided to go to Parepa's home and wait
for her.  He would not let her know he had been to church.  When he
reached the porch he dropped down on the step completely exhausted.  Yes,
he was beaten - and rightly.  The other chap had no chain around his
neck - he did.
        As he sait there in the shadows of the trees he could see  -  Yes,
he was sure - it was Parepa and  --  Joseph.  Something took possession
of him.  He would hide by the side of the porch in the shurbery and
would listen - yes, he would stoop to listen - evesdrop.
        He heard them come up the walk.  'Won't you come in?" Parepa was
saying.  Was hers a sympathetic voice or an understanding one.
        "No, let's just sit down here on the porch," he replied.
        "I'll run in and see if Auntie is all right and then I'll be right
out." she said.
