     "Now what are you up to?  I've already added two years to your
age, isn't that enough for one day?"
     "Oh, it's nothing like that Uncle George, it's just this.  Won't
you please leave all the ugly premiums home next time so those nice
folks will have a good excuse to pick something pretty?  Do please,
just once."
     "Oh, that's just like you, always thinking out a way to get what
you want without letting on.  But I'll try it just once and see how
it works.  They really should have some things they don't need I
suppose."
     "Yes, but they <u>do</u> need them Uncle George.  They need them <u>so</u>
badly but they don't know it.  Maybe you will be the means of finding
the "missing link" in their chain of happines (a phrase she had gathered
from a book).  Who knows?"
     They rode on in silence again for sometime.  Parepa-Roza picturing
little dots of beauty here and there in the many homes they had visited
and Uncle George wondering how in the world he had made such a rash
promise.  Already he could hear the complaint of his customers at not
finding the useful premiums they expected to get.  Well, he'd slip in
a few any way.
