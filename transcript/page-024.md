     What fun it was!  Parepa paraded in front of him in each outfit.
As she did this she wished inwardly that all the folks at home as well
as her school mates could see her now.  Only a day since she left home
and yet how many things had happened.
     "They'll do," said Uncle George as he smiled his pleasure at
Aunt Kate's taste.  "Wish John and Eliza could see her now."
     "Now we'll lie down a while and rest up for tonight." said Aunt
Kate as she took off the last dress over the girls head and carefully
placed it on a hanger in Parepa's closet.  "I'll call you in time,
see if you can't sleep a little."  Parepa laughed at the idea.  She
laid on her bed perhaps ten full minutes before sleep claimed her.
She did not realize that her young body had been greatly taxed by
the "goings on" of the last forty hours.  Wise Aunt Kate knew a rest
was a necessity.  She was anxious that Parepa be at her best that
evening for the picnic and tennis lessons.  Instead of going to bed
herself she slipped down the stairs to the kitchen where she and Uncle
George prepared the lunch for the picnic supper.
     As the work progressed they talked continually of Parepa and her
future.  How they would go about it to convenience John and Eliza that
they should let them have her.  They even planned how her music lessons
and school work could be managed and Kate declared she felt like the
child belonged to her already. ⟦pencil-note: Chap. 4.⟧
     At 5:30 she went to the bed and stood looking down at Parepa for
sometime.  "Our Maude would have been sixteen now", she said half aloud
and half to herself, then she bent over and kissed the warm cheek of
the "sleeping beauty".
