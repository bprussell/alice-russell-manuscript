     Colorado Springs was a delight from the time they arrived at the

the train for Boulder.Manitou with its wonderful mineral springs.,
Pikes Peak which was visited by Allie only, and the many side trips
made by Auto made an ever changing panorama of beauty.
     They arrived in Boulder on Saturday.  The good people into whose
home they were welcomed were cousins of Allie's mother.  A dear old
couple, kindly and entertaining.  They had lived here for many years.
Mr. Teagarden having been the leading real estate man of the City knew
the country like a book.  He had been instrumental in closing more
sales for land and city property than and Realator for miles around.
     When he learned that Johnie was looking for a farm near the
mountains he knew at once the best buy in the country.  It joined a
farm of his own.  Was only a short distance from Boulder and was as
much land as Johnie should handle.  After riding for several days they
decided on this one, providing they could raise the money from their
home and second mortgage to make first payment.
