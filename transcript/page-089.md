     They left the park from the upper entrance.  It was just twilight
when they bade each other good-bye.
     "Did you have a good time?" asked Aunt Kate.
     "Oh, the best time I ever had.  We road and road and the flowers,
the streams, and the sunset were beautiful.  Aunt Kate why am I so
possessed when I see pretty things?  I can hardly tear myself away from
them.  I forget everything else.  I seem immovable."
     "I'm glad you can see the beauty the good Lord gives us so freely.
Lots of us pass by all that, and miss the most precious things in
life," said good kind Aunt Kate.
     "Sit down, Parepa, I want to talk to you about some very important
things."
     "Yes, Auntie, she sang.  "I shall be glad  to listen."
