                              Chapter III

     "Put on your best dress for we are going down town to shop today,"
and Aunt Kate led the way to the bathroom where she carefully dressed
the delighted girl.  She was as pink and white as a lily and her eyes
were dancing with anticipation.  She had always wanted to see the pretty
things in the big stores.  Aunt Kate had her breakfast all fixed on
the kitchen table.  She was not long eating it and was soon waiting
with her Aunt on the corner for a street car.  She enjoyed the ride
down town and was sorry when it ended.
     First, they went to a shoe store.  Aunt Kate explained to the clerk
the kind of "growing girl" shoe she wanted and Parepa-Roza was ushered
to a seat to be fitted.  She whispered to Aunt Kate that she only had
fifty cents in her pocket book but Aunt Kate told her that she and Uncle
George had laid awake last night and planned to play that she was their
own Maude and to get her the things they would like to have gotten for
Maude was she living.
     "She will just wear them". she said to the clerk as the pair she
liked best had been fitted.  They were pretty and well-fitted so Parepa
was much more comfortable than she had been before.
     Next they looked at hats.  A broad brimmed leghorn with a wreath
of daises and roses trailing off the edge of the brim and hanging down
the back was chosen.  Also, a blue and white tam  o'shanter.  Parepa
was so delighted when she looked at herself in the long mirror.
     "She'll just wear it", said Aunt Kate and left the old cheap faded
hat to be delivered.  "Now let's  get some new underwear", and Aunt
Kate steered her into the largest store yet.
