                          CHAPTER XXI

     These two enjoyed many evenings together and through the month of
August they were almost inseparable.  For Parepa's birthday Aunt Kate
and Uncle George planned a six o'clock dinner party.  Mr. Wheeler had
returned from his business trip to the west and would be their guest
that evening.  Uncle Will and Aunt Minnie were invited too.  MrsBreen,
a widow and Mr. and Mrs. Professor Blough completed the party.
     Parepa was delighted with her birthday presents which began to
arrive early in the day.  From Mrs Breen came a beautiful Maracco bound
set of Dickens.  Uncle Will and Aunt Minnie remembered her with a gold
bracelet.  Mr. Wheeler's choice was a large wedge wood vase filled with
eighteen most beautiful red roses.  The card red:  "A rose for every
year.  To wish you joy, my dear." signed, Daddy Wheeler.  Professer
Blough sent her such a beautiful etching, Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata.
Uncle George and Aunt Kate gave her a s⟦unclear: a⟧fty bicycle ⟦unclear: special⟧⟦pencil-delete⟧.  Nothing
came from Frances until late in the afternoon, a pair of beautifully
jeweled Opera glasses nestled in a soft velvet lined leather case.
"With best wishes and a fond memory of an evening with 'Uncle Tom'
and you." - Frances.
     Aunt Kate hired a cateress and assistant to look after the serving
of the dinner.  Ten covers were laid, the appointments were perect.
The table was most beautiful with a dainty birthday cake as center piece.

adorned with garlands of roses told each guest their place.
