Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

its star studded sky seemed to soften the sorrow and loneliness in the
hearts and minds of the earthly companions of the departed one as the
funeral courtage passed into the little grave yard.

     Reverantly as though a throng of mourners were weeping beside the
bier, the body of one whose life had blessed the earth, was laid to rest.

     The last link in the Emmerson chain was broken.  They had taken
their long ride.

        "Ah, love, could you and I with fate conspire,
             To grasp this sorry scheme of things entire;
        Would we not shatter it to bits and then,
             Remould it nearer to the heart's desire?"

                          THE END
