     Allie handed the paper to her husband and sat down in a chair by

     "Do you know who this man is, Mrs Bradford?" the reporter asked.
     "I can think of no man who would be so interested in me and my
family," she answered.  "I must ask you to refrain from questioning me
further.  I do not care to discuss this thing with any one but my
family at present.  I thank you for your interest in me, and if you
will leave your name I will gladly give you the story when there is
anything to tell.  Believe me sincere when I tell you I have answered
your questions honesty," she rose as if to show him out.
