     We now find our Parepa-Roza back with us again, for on the Allen
Club register as well as on the record book of the University is the
name A. Parepa-Roza Wesley, Sparland, Illinois.

     She found lodging in a home across the street from the Club.  There
were five other girls rooming there and a lovely bunch they were.  Parepa
soon became a favorite at the Club as well as on the campus.  She
arranged with the Churches of near by towns to give dramatic entertain-⟦hyphen⟧
ments week ends for which she received one third the receipts at the
door.  This helped the financial end of affairs greatly.  Her ability
as an entertainer was soon recognized by the student body.  She was given
leading parts in pagents and plays as well as finding herself the
physical culture instructor's assistant.  She proved hers⟦strikeover: illegible⟧f champion

Aunt Kate's and (as she thought of him) Frances's deaths.
