     He was a finechild, dark hair like his father's with a touch of

soon identified himself by his strong aversion to nature's food.

     Nothing seemed to agree with him.  For one whole year he gave his
parents a great deal of worry.  They had traded the store for a stock
and fruit farm now and were trying to make the interest on the mortgage
by raising chickens, milking cows and selling fruit.  Richard flourished
on the milk from the herd and grew into a chubby laughing two year old.

     In April, 1915 another baby boy put in his appearance bidding for
