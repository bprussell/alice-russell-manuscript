     I came over this morning to talk with you about the strong
attraction our youngsters have for one another.  I feel sure I'm not
misplacing my confidence when I tell you some things  that have never
passed my lips before.  Frances was born under unusual circumstances.  My
father was Frances's mother guardian.  I was thirty years old when she
died and she was twenty.  I loved this charge of my fathers with all my
heart but on account of the difference in our ages I did not force my
attentions upon her though I was most certain my love was returned.  Some-
times I was sure she wanted me to declare my love.  On account of this
strong attraction I felt for Germany where I studied music under the best
masters for two years.  My heart was not in it and I did not do well.
During this time she was in a finishing college.  Another man fell in
love with her and she imagined herself in love with him.  They sinned as
had her fore-mothers.  Two weeks before they were to have been married
her lover died.  This was eight months before Frances's birth.
