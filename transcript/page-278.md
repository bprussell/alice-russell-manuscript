     Daddy Wheeler came into the room smiling, "So you two old pals
are together so early this morning, eh?" he joked.  "How you feeling
by this time, Frances?"

     "Good!  Only a little weak.  How's yourself?" she said sweetly.

     "Fine!" and the old man stooped and kissed Parepa on the cheek.
"What are we going to do when she goes home?" he said.  "We will surely
miss you.  Won't we Frances?"

     "And I'll miss you both.  I wish you knew my husband and boys.
They are wonderful fellows," she said.

     "I thought of them in the night and how good it  was of them to
let you come to me.  I know it must have been a great sacrifice for them
to give-you up for so long.  Be sure and let them know I appreciate it.
Now I'll tell you why I called you.  I forgot to tell you that you are
to have everything on this place and in the house.  You can do as you
please with them only that I want your boys to have all my masculine
possessions.  My saddle, my watches, rings, guns, cameras, sport goods,
and everything I have that I kept as keep sakes when I came to America
five years ago.  I have them packed just as they were in England for
shipping.  There are eighteen large boxes of them.  I shall order them
taken to the express station soon.  I hope they will get a lot of en-⟦hyphen⟧
joyment out of them," and she smiled as Parepa assured her that they
would.

     "Each one is labeled so they will know their value," she said.  "I
want you to call the housekeeper, the yardman and the butler, please
Daddy.  I want to introduce them to their new mistress."
