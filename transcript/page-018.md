     "Penny for your thoughts", said Frances as he gave her a poke in
the back.  "Wake up and talk to the people".
     "Isn't that a pretty horse", she said and then went to pondering
again.
     "Say, what's-your-name, if you like pretty horses I just wish you
could see some of mine.  My favorite saddle pony is black as coal.
I have a team of pure white ones that Clem scrubs everyday.  I'll show
you their pictures some day.  I'll be glad when I get home and can
ride and drive them again.  You see, I haven't seem them for nearly
two years.  I've been over the ocean that long.  I bet they will know
me though."
     "I bet they can't pull as much as old Coaly and Scamper". she
ventured.  "They can out putt any horses around our place."
     "Maybe, but I bet mine's the prettiest.  What's more, they are
pedigreed."
     "Are they?" said Parepa, not willing to let on that she didn't know
what the word meant.  "I've heard of that kind."
