One evening Johnie came home delighted, kissing his wife he announced
the fact that he had been given a generous raise.  Evidently his boss
had recognized his sterling worth and steadfastness.  Allie could
hardly⟦strikeover: "rdly" overtyped⟧believe it, could be so much.  Shop work was so new to him, she
had thought it would be a long time before he would be given a more
remunerative position.  His new duties were not so strenuous but there
was more responsibility connected with it.

     Everything ran smoothly these days.  Allie had joined the Athenian
Delphian Society, was enjoying the social duties⟦strikeover: "s" overtyped⟧ connected with the
Arcadia Presbyterian Church and the Parent Teacher's Association of the
Columbia School.  She guided her children carefully through the confusing
lower grades and was a real companion to them.  No question however
intricate was passed off unanswered.  In this way they grew through
boyhood with no mysteries of life tormenting their mentalities.
