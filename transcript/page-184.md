     The next afternoon found Parepa boarding the train for Chenoa.
Her visit there was one round of delight.  Her cousins were a jolly lot.
She was feated in grand style.  Was introduced to the elite of the
town and made the usual "crush" on the most eligable young man in town. ⟦pencil-delete⟧

it all but was glad when she found herself back to her studies again.

     She wondered if she were really glad Harold would get back tonight.
One thing she was  sure of, she would not marry anyone until after her
school year was finished.
