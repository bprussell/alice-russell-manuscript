Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hkawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     The Avery Manufacturing Company where he worked was located on
North Adams Street some blocks from their home but Johnie preferred
to walk to and from work saying the exercise and fresh air did him
good.  No doubt they did, but there was another reason.  The car fare
he saved helped to make the monthly payments on their home.  Before
they know it they were overwhelmed with gas, electric, water, paving,
sewer, and tax bills.

     Allie began to wonder what she could do to help out.  She thought
about the offer made her by the furniture Company.  "All you would have
to do," they had said, "is to make yourself thoroughly acquainted with
our stock of curtains, draperies, floor, and wall coverings.  Prepare
yourself to go to the new homes in the city as we direct you,and make
suggestions to the owners of color schemes for the interior decoration
of the whole house.  We will send a man along from the department to
make an estimate of cost."  For this service she was to receive six
dollars a day.

     There would probably be an average of three days a week.  "If I
could go from nine to eleven in the morning and from one to three in
the afternoon I might try it," she thought.  "I couldn't be away when
the children are home from school," she decided so gave up the whole
idea.

     "I wonder if I could study up and give entertainments in the evening
when Johnie is home with the children?  No -o - o," she decided, "Johnie
must have his rest."

     "It will have to be something I can do at home," she pondered, then
the thought came to her why not rent out the spare bed room.  Anyway
she'd try an ad in the evening paper.  She did .  That evening she was
