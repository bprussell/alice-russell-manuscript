     Things had drifted into a regular routine at the Bredford home.
It was always a rush and a hurry from one duty to another both for the
husband with the farm responsibilities and for the wife with children
and household to look after.

     These parents loved their little boys and carefully raised them
as they should be raised.  Nothing concerning their welfare was neglected
as far as they were financially and physically able to supply it.  They
were not over indulgent parents.  There must be a good reason for every
purchase.  Their food, clothing and even their play things were discussed
and their practical value to the child in question made certain before
money was spent for it.

     These were strenuous days.  John was doing his best to fight off
a return of his old trouble.  Finally his Doctor told him he must make
a change.  As long as he was on the farm he would work too hard and not
rest as he should.  Regular hours was what he needed."
