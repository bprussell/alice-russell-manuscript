        Reverend Reynolds, Pastor, as well as the older members of the
church fell to calling Parepa-Roza "Bethany's Angel" in respect for her
sweet voice and helpfulness in the church.
        Aunt Kate had fallen heir to the dreaded malady which claimed her
husband and she begged for the day to come when she might join him.
Parepa took every care of her Aunt refusing the aid of a nurse as she
knew the limit of the Slay finances.  Parepa had saved a small amount from
the recitals and Uncle George's insurance helped some.
        Now the time had come for Frances to return.  Parepa had not told
him of their change of address but had let the Post Office Clerk hunt her
out and forward his letters to her.  She didn't want him to know how
badly they had needed money.
        Frances letters were so filled with the joy of their meeting again
and Parepa and Aunt Kate could hardly wait.  Only a few days now and he
would be here.
        On Sunday mornings a kind neighbor stayed with Aunt Kate while
Parepa went to Sunday School and Church services.  She went to the church
early in order to put some drawings on her blackboard.  As usual Joseph
was there too.  He had some maps to draw on his blackboard.  Their
conversation floated from one side of the room to the other.  Their maps
didn't receive much personal thought as each of them sketched line after
