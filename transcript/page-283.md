     Her maid came in from her morning walk and Daddy Wheeler left the
room.  Parepa lounged lazily on her couch.  Her heart and mind had
grown ripe and were mellowed by the turns of fate.

     Finally sleep came to her closed eyes.  In her dreams she and
Frances were in the boat on the Illinois River in Peoria.  They were
singing love songs.  When the maid roused her to dress for luncheon
she was laying in Frances's arms while the boat drifted where it may.

     "Oh!" she said sleepily.  "My voiceI  I have it again!  It has
come back to me!"

     Her maid thought she was still dreaming but Parepa knew better.
