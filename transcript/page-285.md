                                                                  121

Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

                          CHAPTER XXVI

     "We are going for a long ride tomorrow Parepa.  I'm sure I'm able
now,  You have brought strength to me.  I feel so strong today."

     "Oh, that is grand.  You'll be your old self yet," and saying this
Parepa stooped and kissed the flushed cheek of her first love.

     "Tell me all about your husband and boys.  Business matters have
taken up much of our time.  I know they are the best in the world but
I want to know the intimate homey things about them," and Frances
smiled up at her as her face beamed,, with the pride she felt for her
family.

     "Truly, Frances, I have the kindest and best husband in the world.
My boys are real ones - not a sissy in the bunch," then she told her
the story of their whole married life.  Their joys and sorrows.  Their
little flourishings and hardships.  The story of real life with all
its ups and downs.

     "Oh, if only I could have suffered real hardships.  Your's has been
a real life.  You seem to have gotten a great thrill out of it all,"
laughed Frances.

     "I do now that they are past and gone but they were serious
situations then"and she thanked her God for these "blessings" ahs she
called them.

     "I'm so satisfied now, Parepa.  Every thing I wanted to do before
I left this world is done.  I can go happily now."  The nurse came near
them and Parepa knew their visit was ended for the day.

     Squeezing Parepa's hand she said, "Tomorrow we'll go for a long
ride.  Good bye dear."

     After writing a long letter home Parepa doned her sports suit and
started for a walk.
