comes a time in your life, either early or late, that you want me, or
that I can help you in anyway you can always reach me by addressing me
at Halifax, Nova Scotia.  It will be forwarded to me wherever I am.
Should you have a longing, whether next year or fifty years from now,
to be my companion, remember your home in my heart will be vacant waiting
for you to come live in it.  I shall never love another woman in my
