                              CHAPTER IV

     He was so surprized and happy to see her.  His eyes sparkled as
the love light shone in them.  "'Twas mighty good of you to meet me,
girlie," he said in his old sincere way.  "I thought 'twould be a little
late to call on you at your boarding house so I decided to stay here
tonight and have a glorious day with you tomorrow."

     "I was going away for the Fourth but when I got your letter I
decided I'd come here and meet you and stay at the Hotel myself until
Saturday.  Then I'll go on to Chenoa to visit.  There is really nothing
of interest in Normal for you to see so I thought we'd just have a better
time together here in Bloomington.  Tomorrow we can see the town
together," and she felt that old time chumminess coming back.  It was
nice she thought - but - I must tell him tomorrow.

     They went to the Hotel and each registered for their rooms.  They
washed up then met in the lobby to go for a walk.  They found some seats
to rest in and were soon chatting away about home folks and doings
both really enjoying themselves.

     Parepa unvailing new virtues in her partner as they sat there side
by side in the moonlight.  It was a night that makes lovers, a night
that makes dreams.  John⟦pencil-insert: n⟧ie was dreaming - Parepa was too.  But what
was she dreaming?  She could feel a confusion of dreams.  Finally she
felt an arm around her and a hand turning her face up to -- yes, it
was John⟦pencil-insert: n⟧ie's lips to be kissed.  She sank her head on his shoulders
and sat silently as she passed⟦pencil: pressed⟧ her hand in⟦pencil: into⟧ his to hold.  She was safe

     She could feel his heartbeat.  It was a true heart, a pure heart, a

that's good, not a blot, not a stain on his character.
