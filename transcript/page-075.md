Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

                              CHAPTER XV

     That night Parepa could not sleep.  The first time in her life
she could remember of laying awake, no matter how troubled the day had
been.  She didn't know just why she couldn't sleep - all she knew was
that she was happy, Oh, so happy.
     She heard nothing from Frances for several days.  She caught her-⟦hyphen⟧
self watching the street hoping he would pass and maybe - stop in.
     He didn't - but one day she was called to the 'phone.  It was
Frances.  "Just got back in town," he said.  "What you going to do
tonight?"
     She told him it was choir rehearsal tonight.
     "Can I go, too?" he begged.
     "Surely you can - glad to have you.  Come out early," she said.
     "Alright - 'bout seven - bye,bye."
     "Bye" and she hung up so flushed and smiling that the other girls
teased her.
     "I'm going to tell Mr. Slay," said Carrie and she pointed an
accusing finger at Parepa.
     That night Aunt Kate begged off and stayed home from practice.
     "I always go early to rehearse my solo before the others get
there," she told him as they strolled toward the Church.
     "You don't mean to say you are singing solos already?"  He stopped
still and looked at her.  She was only a child, sweet and innocent as a
rose and so talented.  She was a picture to him - a perfect picture.
The picture of youth and life and  --  love.
     When he sat in a side seat and listened to her sing "The Beautiful
Gates Ajar" he wondered why she practiced.  That song was perfect. No
human being could improve upon it.
