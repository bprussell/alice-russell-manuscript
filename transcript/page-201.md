                              CHAPTER XI

     One afternoon about three o'clock Allie was surprized to see Johnie
come swaying into the house.  "I'm so sick," he said and throwing himself
on the couch he cried, "Call the Doctor quick."  Before the Doctor
arrived Allie had helped him to his bed.
     "It's the "flu" that wicked flue", said the Doctor handling Allie
the termometer to read.  It stood at 105 degrees.  He gave strict orders
for his treatment and left with a grave face.   "Call me at once if he
shows any signs of being worse," he said as he left.
     His head pained him until he could hardly endure it.  Allie did
just as the Doctor directed but at eight o'clock he became delirious.
She called the Doctor.  At that time the flu⟦strikeover: stray letter x-ed out after "flu"⟧ was sweeping the country
from coast to coast.  Doctors drove from place to place for days without
sleep.  They were rushed beyond their strength trying to give relief
to the suffering.
     When he came he had a nurse with him.  "I was afraid of this," he
said.  "He's a very sick man.  We'll do our best to save him."
     Allie had put the boys to bed and had sent Mr. Brown and Rice to
a restaurant for their dinner.  She was almost overcome by the shock of
the afternoon.  Johnie was raving now.  What would she do!  The nurse
worked faithfully but the whole night was a battle.
     Days passed!  Johnie would become concious at times only to
lapse into unconciousness without the least warning.  The young men very
kindly offered to take their meals out until he was better.
