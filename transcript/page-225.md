     Why couldn't we shatter all the pain and suffering in the world?⟦strikeover: end punctuation overtyped on another character⟧
Why must we take whatever fate deals out to us in this world? she
protested in her mind.  Johnie opened his eyes again, "Don't worry
wifie, I'll be all right," he said and went to sleep again.
     Morning light was streaming into the room now.  A nurse offered
Allie a place to rest.  "I'll call you when he wakens," she promised.
The minute her head touched the pillow she was asleep.  She was simply
exhausted from the labor of the day before and the anxiety of the night.
     It was nine-thirty when the nurse said softly, "Mr. Bradford is
awake now."
     Johnie was quite rested and felt comfortable.  He insisted that he
would be all right now and she should go home and not worry.  Allie
knew there was no one to get the meals for the boys so consented.  She
found them cutting the oats as best they could with the old binder.
A trip to town for this or that repair ever so often was necessary.
It was discouraging to say the least.  Never-the-less these boys didn't
give up.  They weren't made of that kind of stuff.
     Some time during  each day Allie and the boys visited Johnie.  He
was doing fine and the Doctor thought would be able for the operation
soon.  He was worried over the expense connected with his sickness.
Allie tried to tell him there was flower money enough to meet it.  How
many things she had counted on doing with the "Flower money" which was
kept by itself in a tin box on her dresser.  There was most three hundred
dollars now.  The insurance was soon due, a tractor payment to be made,
and a note for the last cow purchased, which would be due September first.
