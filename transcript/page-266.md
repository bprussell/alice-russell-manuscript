     After he had gone Parepa turned to Frances and said, "Now darling
het me make you comfortable on your couch," and she took her by the arm
and led her to it.

     "I'm so glad to have that tended to.  Now we can visit and enjoy
ourselves the rest of your stay, can't we dear?" and Frances smiled up
at Parepa.

     "I'm so glad you feel stronger Frances and I hope you will get well
enough to go home with me," she offered.

     "No, I will never be able to travel.  I know my limitations.  I am
happier than I have been since we parted."

     "Oh, Frances, it was so grand of you to deed this wonderful estate
to me.  I fell in love with it at sight and I'm going to love it as
long as I live."

     "I'm glad you will.  It has been a real home to me.  I would like
to have you keep three of my servants here as I know they can be trusted
to care for the place.  I have looked out for them well, with that
understanding," and she smiled again at Parepa.

     "You haven't forgotten anything that will make it easy for me,
Frances.  I don't understand how you could be so thoughtful of every
little detail."

     "It's all I've had to do these last five years.  I have given my
whole time to it and enjoyed every minute I spent doing it.  Some day
while you are here I'm sure I'll be able to show you around the place.
