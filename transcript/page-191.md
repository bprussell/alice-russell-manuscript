Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     Kind relatives and neighbors did all they could do to help with
the housework and farm work, but Allie was determined not to allow one
dose of medicine or one little errand⟦strikeover: stray character under the "d"⟧ for Johnie to be done by anyone
but herself⟦strikeover: letters overtyped⟧.  Sher made her bed on a cot beside him and so timed
herself to his regular needs that she wakened involuntarily at the
appointed time.

     Days passed - weeks passed.  The fluid was gone from the heart
linings only to appear in the linings of the lungs, first one and then
the other, then back to the heart.  Consultations of Doctors sere often
held.

     His condition at no time was such that he could stand the shock of
having the fluid drawn from his body.  Months passed - Allie was growing
pale.  She was looking worn and worried.  The Doctor's were pleased
with the way their patient was cared for, but they felt concerned
about Allie now.  She must have some rest.  Johnie knew she must too.
He would try to give up to having a stranger take her place in caring
for him.  She had been wonderful - she must rest now.

     The Doctor suggested it to her.  "If I am not doing it well I
will gladly give up to someone else but I like caring for him myself,"
she said.

     "Well, we'll try a nurse for a couple of weeks anyway.  That will
give you a chance to catch up on your rest," said the kind old Doctor.

     It was not only the care of her husband that was telling on her.
It was the fact that almost the last hog of the car load had died with
cholera.  Their best milk cow and baby calf died.  The fall pigs were
being burned by the pile each day and grain prices had sunk to a low
level.  All this Allie had carefully kept from Johnie.
