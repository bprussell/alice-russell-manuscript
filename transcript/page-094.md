                                                                   92
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "No, I will never be ready.  All I want to know is this.  Did you
ever kill anybody?" she asked.
     "No, oh no.  You have the wrong idea."
     "Did you ever steal?"
     "No, dear, you don't understand."
     "Did you ever love a woman before?"
     "Oh no, never, and I never will but let me tell you --"
     "Did you ever bear false witness?  Now think hard, Frances."
     "No, darling, never.  You don't understand at all."
     "I don't care to understand anything more.  Oh, Frances, can't we
go on as we are, never giving a thought to your past whatever it may
be?  Can't you see it is you - just you - that I love.  <u>Please</u> promise
me you will never refer to this again."
     "I wish I could, darling.  Oh God, how I wish I could, but it is
not only my past but my present and future.  For your sake I must tell
you.  It's here, It's all here,"and he pulled from his breast a large
gold locket fastened around his neck with a long thin gold chain.  It
glistened in the moonlight as he dangled it before her.  "It was my
mother's," he said.
     "Put it back, put it right back.  Kepp your secret but give me
all your love,take mine in return and I shall be happy.  Oh, so happy,
Frances."
     "I would give anything in the world if I could take your love on
those terms but I can't, I won't!" he said.
     "Please!  Oh please!  do not mention this again.  She, Frances,
look, here comes the Bawled Eagle.  Let's ride the waves," and she
clapped her hands in glee.
     "Fine!" and he took the oars and rowed near the big excursion boat.
