     The next day Aunt Kate called her brother-in-law, Mr. Wm Slay,
to her home for a private talk about Parepa's future.  "If she takes up
dramatics she will necessarily have to have at least two hours more a

     "She has certainly made good there," he said.  "But I will try to
make arrangement where by she can have an extra hour in the morning and
get off earlier in evening."  "I had hoped to raise her wages soon but
can't if I give her more time."
     "No, we wouldn't ask you to do that.  We don't charge her any

calls them will be high as we want her to have the best teacher possible."
     Mrs. Rose Mendenhall Breen is the best private instructor in the

     On Saturday morning after Parepa had gone to the shop Uncle George
and Aunt Kate started out to find a suitable teacher for Parepa.  They
found Mrs Breen much interested in taking her but the lessons would
have to be given twice a week in one hour periods.  They were to cost
two dollars per lesson.  She was to be given a complete course which
would require a great deal of home study.  They went home to talk it
over with Parepa before deciding.
