                                                                  114

Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     Parepa slept peacefully all night.  Aunt Kate made a bed on the
floor beside her and got some rest.
     Frances didn't sleep or even try to.  He wished he could sail out
of her life forever.  How did 'he ever allow himself to become so attached
to any girl.  In all his life he had managed to side step the advances of
the many fair maidens of his own class as well as the well directed
management of the mothers.  He was termed as "a little odd" but desirable
in society.  He detested public demonstrations and avoided attention at
all times.
     Mr. Wheeler was his good counselor always.  No one knew the amount
of his wealth.  It was a great subject of discussion.  So well did Mr.
Wheeler manage his affairs⟦strikeover: first "a" overtyped on another letter⟧, both financially and personally, that no one
place could claim him as a resident or be sure it boasted the habitation
of his bank roll for any length of time.  If the least familiarity began
to exist in one location they would move on to another.  He education had
been gathered in many different countries.  He hoped he would "finish" in
America.  He lay awake now wondering what was the best thing to do concern-⟦hyphen⟧
ing this wonderful young girl whose trust he knew he shouldn't encourage
longer.
     Such were the pangs of fate.  The sins of the parents visited upon
their children.  He hated money.  It could not buy what he would have
given it all to own.  The study of the science of life had brought home
to him many facts that could not be denied.  He had long since determined
never to propagate his kind.  The time had come for his chain to be
broken.
     It was his mothers wish, too.  Five generations of mothers had
given their lives to their new born, or in his case unborn babies.  It
must stop - and it would stop.  There would be no  more
                                                  lives lost -
