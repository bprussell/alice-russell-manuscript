     "She seemed perfectly at ease.  I couldn't notice the least thing

     "No, just do as you like.  I expect you are tired," and he waved
a good bye to her.  When she reached her room she felt relieved not to
find her maid there.  She wanted time to remove her jewelry first.
She placed one after another in the case,a queer feeling came over her
as she counted the sparkling diamonds in the necklace.  "Our good times"
she said and closed the lid.  This fifty year old Parepa was as happy
as any sixteen year old girl ever dared to be.  She was sure her voice
had come back to her while Frances was putting the jewelry on her.
Nanettezen came in.  After she had changed Parepa's attire to one of
new comfortable lounging robes she asked for the evening off.

     "Certainly you can," and Parepa was glad again that she would be
left alone.  In the deep silence of her room her thought fell to memories
of the past.  She wondered if it was right for her to guard them so
sacredly.  She was sure she did not love Frances in the same way she did
her husband.  That kind of love died long before her love for Johnie
was born.  What was it then, that annoyed her.
