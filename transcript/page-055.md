     "I'll go up with you," and Aunt Kate tucked her in her bed and
kissed her a loving good night and crept quietly down the stairs,
glad the day was over for the young girl.
     "Poor little thing - she doesn't realize the first twangs of
womanly instinct are working in her heat.  She's really in love with
Frances but she don't know just what is the matter with her," and Aunt
Kate sat down real close to Uncle George whose arm some way got 'Round
her waist just as it had 'bout twenty years before.  Their eyes met
their lips met and once again they were  way back when".
     Here they sat, not speaking a word but living again the old days
and the new.  In memory they were in youth again.  The lights were
low and so wrapped were they in their own thoughts that the sharp
ring of the door bell startled them into the present.
     Uncle George went to the door to find Frances and Mr Wheeler
there.
     "Come in, come in", cordially invited Mr. Slay.
     "We can't stay but a minute.  I couldn't leave without saying

is Mr. Wheeler, my guardian."
     "Where's Parepa?" and Frances looked into the dining room.
