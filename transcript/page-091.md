Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "Now Aunt Kate don't do all your planning for my pleasure.  Frances
is leaving for England in September and I want to enjoy August with him.
You know, Aunt Kate, he and I have the best times together.  Like
tonight - he was such good company.  Isn't it strange that he and I ever
met?  It just ahppened you know that he boarded across the street."
     "He said tonight that he has never found a friend he liked as well
as he does me, and I know I'll never find one I'll like as well as I do
him.  You just happened to ask him over to supper that night, you know,
because you felt sorry for him."
     "Yes, smiled Aunt  Kate, "he helped me home from the grocery store
with my packages one day and I became interested in him.  He was so
mannerly and attractive.  After that I always took pains to talk with
him when I met him on the street.  That's how strangly things come about
in this world.  Did he ever tell you anything about himself, Parepa?"
     "No, Aunt Kate, but I believe he wants too and I think I'll let
him some day.  Every time he hints about himeself I change the subject.
Some way I don't want to know.  It might make a difference you know."
     "Yes, dear it might, but if it will the sooner you know it the
better.  There is undoubtedly a mystery somewhere.  Neither he nor Mr.
Wheeler tell much of their past.  Do you know how old he is, Parepa?"
     "No, Auntie, I don't.  He never says and I never think to ask him.
I will though someday."
     "Now, let's go to bed, dearie, and I hope you will dream sweet
dreams.  Good night," and with a tender kiss she left her.
