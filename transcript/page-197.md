Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

                              CHAPTER X

     Their farm was sold and with the little money realized above the
first mortgage and the second which they took themselves they made a
first payment on a six room home in a new addition to Peoria called
Hanssler Place.

     It was great fund⟦pencil-delete⟧ furnishing this new home.  Allie studied long
over draperies, and floor coverings.  She had to be so very careful not
to over step her limited funds alloted for this purpose.  However, when
the job was done it proved the artistic nature of this homemaker and
mother.  Every room showed definite planning.

     When the man came from the drapery department of Peoria's largest
store to hang the living room drapes he showed his surprize and pleasure
at the wonderful color combinations throughtout the house.  "You are
primarily an artist, Mrs Bradford," he told her.  "Do you mind my bring-⟦hyphen⟧
ing our manager to look this over?"

     She consented and the next morning they arrived.  The manager was
genuinely enthusiastic over the"completed picture" as he called it.  "It
is unusual indeed to find such harmony throughout the whole house.
Would you consider an offer from us, Mrs Bradford?  We are greatly in
need of someone like you who understands color and its effect.  I'm
sure we could make you an interesting proposition."

     Allie was  greatly pleased but reminded him of her family, "I'd
love doing it," she said, "but you know three lively boys keep me
busy.  It would be out of the question."

     "Think it over," he said as they left the house.  "We'll make it
worth your while."

     Robert was nine, Richard six, and Arthur four years old now.  John
was fortunate in finding not too strenuous employment at once.
