     "Alright just as you say, but for my part I'm just as well

     At dinner he spoke again of Frances.  "He is such a fine, jolly
young fellow - clean looking chap and especially well mannered, yet
he don't spoil everything by "showing off".  I'm going to try to break
through the ice enough to learn more about him."
     "Now if I were you, I'd let things run along as they are and not
worry about his private affairs.  That his business not ours."
     "Oh, sure it is, sure it is", said her husband, "guess I'm over
curious."
     Sunday morning found the four of them in their pew at Church.
