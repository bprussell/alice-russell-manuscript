                                                                    71
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "It is signed, J.C. Wheeler, Sec."
     "Oh, yes," she gasped.  "Thank you so much," and she hung up the
receiver.
     When she faced her family four pair of inquiring eyes were turned
to hers.  Before she could answer their unspoken question the 'phone
rang again.
     "Peoria calling," said the operator.  "Go ahead please."
     "Hello, is this you, Allie?"
     "Yes, it is I."
     "Well, say," the voice went  on, "have you seen tonight's paper?"
     "No, I haven't."
     "Well, some one in Arigona is looking for you.  There's a long piece
about it.  Can you imagine who it can be?"
     Allie recognized the voice of an old schoolmate whom she knew to
have developed into a full fledged gossip.  Of all people she was one
not to be confided in.
     "I expect there are a lot of folks there," she fenced.
     "Yes, but this must be a man and I bet he's rich.  The piece in
the paper reads as if he might be going to die and wants to leave you
something.  Wouldn't that be grand?
     "Oh, I'm afraid you are letting your imagination run away with you.
I'm sure there is no man hunting me,"  she answered quickly.
     "Well, if it didn't cost so much to talk over long distance I'd
take time to read you what it says in the paper.  Really I must hang up
now before I run up a bill.  Let me know all about who it is and what
he wants when you find out.  Will you Allie?"
     "Thank you so much, Bess.  Goodbye," said Allie and she turned
again to face inquiring eyes.
