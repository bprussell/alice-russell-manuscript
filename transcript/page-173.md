     He was so very uneducated, not especially attractive, and not as
well groomed as most of the young fellows in their crowd.  Allie was
not sure that it wasn't all these things she admired most in him.  He
did nothing to mislead her yet she felt he understood her better than
anyone else.

     After talking it over they decided to let the matter rest until
she returned from school.  Perhaps she would know her mind better by
that time.  They'd just keep on being good friends for awhile.
