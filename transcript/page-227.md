Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "'Twas nothing but their untiring faith that saved him," said the
old Doctor just as all the Doctors before him had said.
     The winter passed - Spring was being welcomed in by the lengthening
days, the singing of birds and the April showers.  Tulips⟦strikeover: third letter "l" overtyped on "b"⟧ and narcissus
were springing from there winter beds and preparing for their grand bow
to the hurrying motorists passing by.  Once again Johnie and Allie were
alive to the work that lay before them.
     The boys had taken the responsibility of the farm into their own
hands, and were willing to put their time and strength against the
weather,insects, grain prices ,and politics of another year.  Again this
family was up before daylight working willingly and trustingly until
set of sun hoping and praying that their efforts would be rewarded
substantially when the harvest was over.
     Everything they owned but the dahlia bulbs was mortgaged.  They
gave these securities willingly for it was not their intention to best
anyone.
