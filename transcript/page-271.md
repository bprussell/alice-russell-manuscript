     "You are to wear it this afternoon.  It is Miss Emmerson's wish,"
she said.

     "All right," Parepa answered.  "I'd do anything to please her.  Is
there any jewelry to be worn with it?" she asked remembering the cheap
beads (one of Uncle George's tea ticket premiums) she had worn that
night.

     "There is none here," she answered and the subject was dropped.

     "It is one-forty-five now and you are due for luncheon at two.
Just let me change your pumps," so saying she slipped the new nude
pumps on Parepa's feet.  The messenger was clearing the room of what
boxes were there and the modistes were leaving.

     "You must wear the hat to match, Mrs Bradford.  She wants you
to," and so saying pulled the soft filmy thing over her beautifully
dressed white hair.  Touching a little soft powder to her face she said,
"There now, you are ready.  May I go down with you.  I would like to
witness Miss Emmerson's pleasure?" she pleaded.

     "Of course you can," and taking her maids arm they descended the
stair.  Before they reached the second step Parepa noticed the forms
of two men standing at the foot looking up.  One was Daddy Wheeler, the
other - Could it be possible - was ⟦unclear: Frances⟧!  Dressed and looking like
him old self.  His eyes shining, his cheeks flushed.

     "Frances!  Can it be you?  Can it be you are able to do this -this
joke?" she said, wondering what her maid would think, but Nanettzen had
turned and was going to her room.
