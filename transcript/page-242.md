Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     That depends upon the kind of people they are.  Does this man

     "Then he remembered," she thought.  "Yes, the same old Daddy
Wheeler.  Let's see  --  he must be eighty-two or three years old by
this time."  Then patting his cheek tenderly she said,  He was Frances's
guardian and Secretary then too.  I'll tell⟦strikeover: first letter double-struck; reads like "bell"⟧ you all I know if you will

voice with a quality in it she had never heard before.
     "Can he be jealous?" she thought.  She began at the beggining.  The
first night she met Frances at Aunt Kate's supper table.  She took him
through the five years of their acquaintance.  Of their love and
devotion, then the fact that Francis could never be the father to her
children, and the parting never to meet again.  She had never told this
before.  Then kissing her husband as she lay in his arms she could fell
