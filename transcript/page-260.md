Then, you were so brave when we parted.  That gave me added determination.
I was bound to go on doing all the good I could where ever I could.  I
have not increased my holdings since then.  You remember all the Emmerson
money was originally made on turpentine.  There is not a stain or taint
on it.  It was made by fair dealing.  You will never have to apoligize
for it," her eyes beamed.  "My passing will close a book which only you
and Daddy Wheeler have read.  Let me pass quietly.  I have given my
orders.  I do not want any publicity."

     Daddy Wheeler entered the room with a stack of official looking
envelopes in his hand.  "Everyone of them is ready for you signature,
Frances," he said, "but you must rest until after noon."  The nurses
entered and rolled her from the room.

     "Now, Parepa, we will go over these together so you will understand
the business perfectly," and Daddy Wheeler drew his chair closer to hers.
He explained to her the process of transfering the bank deposits from
Frances's name into hers.  "You won't have a bit of trouble taking care
of them.  The interest on each is payable yearly and they are divided
up so some are due the first of every month.  The banker will forward
you a draft for the interest and you can deposit them wherever you care
to.  Your income will amount to a great deal per year.  It is her wish
that you be not restricted in any way.  It is yours to do as you like
with.  However, she has put to your checking account in four banks the
amounts of two hundred and fifty thousand dollars for your immediate
needs.  Here are the bank books and checks.  You can use them immediately.
They are already in your name.
