     "You will not be able to visit with your friend more today," the

     "You better stroll about the grounds a bit Parepa," said Frances.
"Goodbye for today," and with a kiss they parted.  Parepa did as
Frances suggested.  She walked for over an hour through the entrancing
gardens.  Once a awhile she would come upon a group of men working at
some duty or another on the estate.  It all seemed like a dream to her
now.  She could not seem to grasp the reality of the thing.  As she went
into the house, the thought came to her, "This is my house, what will
I do with it."

     The evening passed and the morning broke fair and fine.  In the
distance the irrigated land showed signs of vegetation.  Parepa sat for
a long time studying the view from her window.  The panorama of beauty
almost took her breath away.  She knew this tract of raw desert land
had been selected and purchased by Frances and Daddy because of its
natural beauty and suitability to their needs.  From her windows she
raved over the incomparable beauty of the colorful mountains.  Their
musical names - Santa Ritas, Tortillitas, Santa Catalinas, and Rincons
interested her greatly.  She knew they were steeped in tradition, history,
romance, and adventure.  She hoped she would learn a little of their
story while there.  And to think this place is mine.  It was beginning ⟦unclear: do⟧
