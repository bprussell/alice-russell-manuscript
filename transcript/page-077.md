Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "Wish I'd happened along about tht time, Goldie Locks," he
teased.  "But seriously its time you gave all your attention to your
talents."
     "I have the whole month of August off duty at the shop and am not
going to study one thing during that month.  Won't that seem funny?
Just going to play and play all month," she laughed.
     "Yes, and I know some one that's going to play and play with you,
too.  That is, if you will let me", and he squeezed her hand tight.
     "Alright, what will we play?  Let's plan it all out.  I love to
ride horse back and swim and row a boat and play tennis and just stroll
and stroll and stroll around in beautiful Glen Oak Park.  I love
everything beautiful, Frances, and I can't control my passionate love
for God's flowers.  Wasn't it grand of him to put so many of them in
the world for us to enjoy?  I'm just possessed by their beauty and
fragrance.
     "I love beautiful things, too, but most of all beautiful lives ⟦pencil-insert: —⟧
like yours, you know.  I'm not surprized that you love all these things.
It's just what I'd expect of you."
