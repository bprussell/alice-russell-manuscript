     "But, I'm big enough and everyone thinks I'm sixteen or seveteen."

     "Your pretty young, my girl, but I'll see what can be done about
it."
     This was enough of a promise for now.  Parepa-Roza could wait
now that the subject was broached.  But all the way to the city she
kept her thoughts on her "job".
     Finally Uncle George broke in with, "Now, just what do you think
you could do in a big city?"
     "I could learn to do most anything if I got paid for it."  And
Parepa-Roza's thoughts went back to all the pretty things she had
wanted in her young life but there was only funds for the bare
neccesities and no extras for anyone
     Uncle George's mind was busy too.  "Gosh, she's a pippin', having
all this tucked away in her head and never peeping a word of it to any-⟦hyphen⟧
body.  Nice little think too, wouldn't Kate be tickled to death to
have her stay with us.  I can just see her now dolling her up in
some of her clothes made over to fit her.  Kate's handy that way."
