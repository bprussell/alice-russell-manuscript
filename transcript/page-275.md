     "Of course I do if you want me to have it, but I wonder if you

     "Yes, it would.  I'll do that," he said as matter of fact as if he
were deciding which apple he would eat.  They were in the library now.
Daddy Wheeler was waiting for them.

     "Well, my children, did you get enough to eat?" he laughed.
looking at Frances he asked, "How did you stand the exertion?"

     "Fine!  I have felt fine and had a most wonderful time,"  A nurse
entered and laughed as she asked Parepa if they went through their old
play all right.

     "Yes, without a mistake," she answered.  "Frances had a wonderful
memory."

     "I'm sorry to have to interrupt now, but don't you think it best
for you to change your theatrical garb and take your rest?  You know
you haven't taken a dozen steps a day for six weeks.  If you don't abuse
yourself maybe Doctor will let you try it again soon."

     As Frances hesitated she said, "Come now, I know Mrs Bradford will
excuse you."

     "Oh, certainly I will.  It's been wonderful to have been together
so long."  She rose to go to her room stooping to kiss Frances she said,
"Goodbye until tomorrow.  Get a good rest and be ready for a new day.
Thanks for our good time.  Can I take my hat off now?" she laughed.
