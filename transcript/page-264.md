     "Thank you, that's nice of you to say that.  Now I think I'll write
a letter home," she said and sat about it.  It was a long loving letter.
She did not think it important to tell them of the financial transactions
of the morning.  "Frances has looked out for our future finances

letter Nanettezen took it to a servant and it was sent at once to the
Post Office.

     At two o'clock she and Daddy dined together in a little portico.
Parepa was delighted with the light heartedness of her companion.  He
joked and laughed with her as one many years his junior.  Finally he
asked, "Do you like it here, Parepa?"

     "Oh, yes, I do." she said.  "I only wish my husband could see it.
He'd love this setting.  He's crazy about mountains," she said.
