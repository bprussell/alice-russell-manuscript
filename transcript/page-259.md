     "It is best that you didn't.  I have had a lesson in real life that
is priceless.  If you really want me to have this money I am sure I have

good, for having felt the need of it myself," she assured him.

     "You taught me many years ago that 'The Earth is the Lord's and the
fullness thereof'.  I never forgot the many tales you and I had together
on this subject.  Being convinced that you were right, I have used my
surplus income from this money each year for Charities.  So far as
possible I have made these gifts anonymous.  I have been so happy in
doing this that I feel no reproach against the life my mother chose for
me.  Our line will soon be broken Parepa.  It is best that it should be.
You are the one I have chosen to carry on for me.  I am giving you this
money for your own, to do as you wish with it.  I have only one request.
That is that you first, build for yourself a home such as you and I
talked of thirty years ago.  I want this home to cost you at least
five hundred thousand dollars.  It is the only monument I want - just
your home.  That may seem extravagant to you but you will find it
easy to use that much on grounds and buildings and don't forget our
lake, our rose arbors, our wonderful trees.  I have never forgotten
your love for them all,"and again they sat silently holding hands.  Their
memory chasing over the past years together.

     "I'll try to carry out your every wish, darling," and Parepa
kissed the hand she held tenderly.  "But what is there in this world I
can do for you in return," she asked.

     "I am the debtor dear," she answered.  "I can never repay you for
the slant on life you gave me.  Of course, you didn't know it but you
