                              CHAPTER XV

     Gardening time had come.  This was  the greatest pleasure Allie
could ask for.  She loved her garden.  Every woman needs a hobby especially
in the middle years of her life after her big job of raising her family
is done.  Allie's hobby was her garden.  In its changing blooms she
created the expression of the beauty and tranquillity which life had so
often perversely denied her.  She craved it so hungerily.
     This farmer's wife, who is primarily an artist, planted her very
soul in the seed bed and the flowers that sprang from it represented
her moods and manners for she used her garden, not alone as a comfort
and pleasure, but as an exhaust or safety value.  When things indoors
became boresome, when the boys were on a tear, running through the house
throwing water on each other; when the cake feel, when the gasolene
engine refused to run the washing machine then Allie wisely turned her
back on it all and rushed to her garden.  How she uprooted the weeds!
In every place a root had been she dropped a bit of temper.  After having
buried her troubles she satisfies her better nature by transplanting
and nursing the baby plants.  Before she returns in the house she is
feeling like a different woman.  Her grouch is gone and she goes about
making the best of things; really enjoying her work.
     She had a definitely planned garden this year.  It was planned with
the idea of commercializing on it.  She had the most of it to do with
her own hands as the men folks were busy comtinually in the fields.
They were all working hard from daylight until dark these days.  The
crops and garden looked good and their hopes were raised to the highest
pitch.
