Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     Day after day passed and these two lovers were the talk of the
Club and campus.  One fact was clear, neither was making much headway
with their learning.  Harold didn't volunteer any information as to
his home life nor did Parepa think of it.  All she thought of was this
wonderful fellow that was to be her husband.

     Theirs was a made love - that "sweeps one off their feet" so to
speak.  They wasted no time.  They were together always out of school
hours.  It was the day before Fourth of July, Parepa's cousins had
invited her to spend the Fourth and week end with them at Chenoa, Illinois
Harold was going to the southern part of the state to spend the holiday
with home folks.

     It was going to be a fearful separation.  Just think!  four days⟦pencil-insert: !⟧
How would they ever stand it?  The goodbyes were said at last and Harold
waved from the platform of the coach as far as he could see Parepa's
handkerchief in the air over her head.

     When she returned to her room to pack for her own trip her eye
fell on an envelope, the hand writing of which she recognized at once
as that of Johnie's.  She held it in her hand a long time before opening
it.  Then she flung herself on the bed and tore it open as if quilty of
some sneaking crime.

     She read in silence for awhile, then she started with an "Oh,
what shall I do?  What shall I do?  Johnie says he's coming over here
for the Fourth.  He is on his way now.  I can't stop him," and she
wrung her hands as she walked the floor.  "He will be in Bloomington
tonight.  I can't go off and not see him."  She buried her head in her
pillow and cried as a hurt child, "It's my fault!  I should have told
him," she cried to herself.
