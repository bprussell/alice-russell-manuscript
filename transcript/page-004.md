     It was not always mischief that was on this child's mind.  Often
questions arose that were almost incredible for such a young mind.
She invariably chose to figure the answers out for herself.
     She was a very attentive church goer, never missing a meeting
'though she with the other five Wesleys usually had to walk a mile
each way unless some kind neighbor offered them a ride in their
surry, as was often the case.
     They liked to question this young girl for when she answered,
as one neighbor said of her, "she always says something".
     She grew finally to a beautiful girl of fourteen.  When she
finished grade school the question arose in the family just what was
best to do with her.  There were two sisters and one brother older
and one brother and sister younger than she.  The family finances,
