                                                                   10

Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

a lot of those pretty pictures for the walls and curtains, and sofa
pillows, and vases!  Lots of vases for flowers and those dear little
lace collars to make their dresses pretty and where you've got a
spitoon let's put those comb and brush sets for ladies, and, OH!
what's that, Uncle George?" and Parepa-Roza most fell off the seat
she was so delighted with what met her eyes.
     "Oh, that's Glen Oak Park", said Uncle George in a most causal
way.
     "Oh!" gasped Parepa-Roza leaning forward on the seat, "Isn't it
pretty?  Do you suppose Heaven is anything like that?"
     "Don't know, never been there." and Uncle George wondered what
the next question would be.  There was no more.  Parepa-Roza was so
wrapped up in her thoughts that she was most thrown from the seat
again when Uncle George pulled up the reins and the horses stopped
short.
     "Well, here we are Parepa-Roza" he said.
     "I'm not Pepper-Rosie, I'm Alice Parepa-Roza, sixteen years old
<u>and don't you forget it."</u>
     Then the door opened and out came Aunt Kate; pretty, sweet, and
gushing.
     First she kissed Uncle George, a big smack and then gathered her
young visitor, as best she could, into her arms.
     "Oh, it's Alice Parepa-Roza!" she exclaimed.  "How glad I am you've
come, and what a nice big girl you've grown to be."
     "Yes," said Uncle George, "pretty good size for a sixteen year old,
eh. Kate?"
     Parepa-Roza could have kissed him for that.
