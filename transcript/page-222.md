work until dark every night, day after day, week after week, month after
month.  Not one cent had gone for cigars, drinks, unwholesome amusements
or riotous living in any form.
     What were they to do?  They willingly turned over all the money
they had raised to their creditors just as they had done for years but
the bank saw fit to start foreclosure on their farm mortgage.  After
employing a lawyer to advise them it was thought best to sign the farm
over to the bank.  This left them without a farm of their own, but still
plenty of debts.  If they had been the kind of people who give up to
discouragements they certainly would have "folded their tents" as the
Arabs and as silently stole away.
     Not so, this family.  They rented another eighty acre farm near them
making two hundred and forty acres in all and set about to try again.
The garden brought⟦strikeover: "r" overtyped on another letter, possibly "f"⟧ early returns in cash.  The list of contracts signed

     Rock garden plants found a ready sale, too.
     Johnie gave most of his time  to cultivating and transplanting the
