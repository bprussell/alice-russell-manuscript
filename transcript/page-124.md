Submitted by:

Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     Anna Bell, my father, the nurse, myself, and two of America's best
physicians sailed for Glascoe on August 12th..
     On August 15th. while on the ship she sent for me and told me as
calmly as you and I are talking of her love for me.  How she had craved
my love as we roamed the gardens, rode together over the estate, read
together and played together.  She told me how she had hoped I'd give u p
the big brother idea and grab her in my arms and cover her with real
love kisses.  It nearly killed me.  I hardly heard her as she said quietly,
"Kiss me.  Then call the Doctors and nurse now, my baby, <u>our</u> baby, your's
and mine wants to see the world.  I give it to you, wrapped in its flesh
and blood is all my love.  Take it and keep it forever."
     I scarcely remember another thing until two days later, August 17th.,
twenty years ago a beautiful baby - my baby - was brought to me and placed
in my arms.  The letter which was to be given me with it lay on its
blanket.
     'Shall I leave you alone?' the nurse asked.
     'Yes,' I answered.  Oh, the miserable joy I felt in the warmth of
that little human.  I ⟦strikeover: hoped⟧⟦pencil: opened⟧ the letter and read its message for I know
the mother's life had fled even though no one told me.  I can never repeat
what I read as I scanned the most astounding request ever made by a
mother.  <u>It Shall be</u> -- <u>it shall be</u>! I cried as I paced the floor with
this, my new born son in my arms."
