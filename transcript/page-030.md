                                                                    29
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

Kate throwing herself in her arms and kissing her.  "You and Uncle
George are so good to me, but what can I do for you?" she asked looking
troubled.
     "Just be a <u>good</u>, <u>good</u> girl always and we shall feel as if we had
been more than repaid". and Aunt Kate patted her tawny head lovingly.
     This girl had everything to learn.  Her simple trust in people, her
untarnished character, her winsome ways and sweet disposition together
with a quick alert mind had completely won the hearts of her middle
aged Uncle and Aunt.  She was so unspoiled in everyway and was beginning
to disclose a very witty and jovial temperment.
