     "Now you must sleep as long as you can in the morning because
you are all worn out from your long ride, and you know we are going
to have lots of fun tomorrow."
     After the light was snapped out and Parepa left alone she reviewed
the days happenings.  I didn't suppose so much could happen in so short
a time.  I must ask⟦strikeover: blotted overtyped character after "ask"⟧ Aunt Kate tomorrow what country pedigreed horses
are from and what they look like.  Hope Uncle George won't kiss me
tomorrow night.  Wonder if I'll make the salad to--m--o--rr--ow  but
the child was asleep, not to awaken until the sun was high in the sky.
She couldn't believe she had slept so late.
     Aunt Kate heard her and came running upstairs calling out, "Sleepy
head, sleepy head", and as she entered the room Parepa noticed she was
dressed to go out.
     "How's our girl this morning?" she asked.
     "Fine, but how late is it Aunt Kate?"
     "Nine-thirty, dear, I'm glad you slept so well."
     Parepa couldn't remember of ever having slept so late.
