     "Why didn't you tell us, Mr. Wheeler, and we would have had two
birthday cakes,' asked Mrs Slay.
     'We did not intend ever to tell you but since these two are so
wrapped up in each other I felt I should come to you before we leave and
tell you, who have been so kind to this young girl as few of the facts as
they exist."
