     Allie most collapsed.  How could he stand another operation she
wondered, then remembering the super-human endurance he had shown in
times before she took heart again.  After Robert had bid his father
goodbye and left for home and labor in the fields Allie sat beside her
husband's bed quietly watching him as he slept from the drugs given him
for that purpose.
     A review of their whole life together passed through her mind.  She
studied her husbands features carefully.  The world would not call them
beautiful yet Allie knew the moulding of every line of expression on
that kindly face had been sculptured there by loves chisel.  He did not
dwell on his troubles, blunders, losses or suffering long enough to
permit a plastic impression of these unhappy things to be carved into
his countenance.  About his lips played the sure curves of strength and
purpose.  He was just a clean gentle everyday man who did his work
gladly and happily each day.  He had not a college trained mind but an
educated heart and a graceful bravery which so many lack.
     He turned his head on his pillow now and opening his eyes smiled
faintly at Allie.  Then putting his hand in hers he fell to sleep again.
It wasn't a soft well manicured hand, instead a callosed rough one.  The
hand of one who cultivates and creates from the soil.  Fingers of love
and understanding were stretched out to touch her own, that better
courage come to her.  As she sat quietly thinking these old familiar
words came to her.
