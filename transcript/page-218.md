                                                                    54
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     Allie treasured the companionship of her many friends in the
Church, Delphian Society, Woman's Club and Home Bureau.  Johnie never
feeling quite up to keeping in step with the men of his age in their
many activities was in sympathy with all movements that made for a
better community.  His quiet genial nature made a friend for him of
every acquaintance.  Sickness, hard luck, and worry had taken their toll
from both husband and wife.  Their staunch sticktuitiveness and refusal
to be beaten down was most astonishing.  The successive come-backs after
each serious illness was little short of miraculuous.
     "Life is so sweet even at its worst⟦strikeover: final letter overtyped⟧," Johnie would say, "I'm going
to hang on to it as long as I can."
     It was the middle of April now.  The clicking of the oat seeder
and the put put put of the tractor could be heard on every side.  Young
chickens were being hatched and demanded lots of care.
