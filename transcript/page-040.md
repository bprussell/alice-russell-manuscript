                                                                    38
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

Aunt Kate went through the practice hour together and then decided to
take a short walk.  As they turned the corner someone sprang between
them grabing both by the arm and laughing out, "I caught you this
time."   It was Frances, of course.
     "Where are you headed for?" he asked.
     "Just out for a walk around the block," said Aunt Kate.  "Where
are you going yourself?" she asked.
     "Oh, just wherever you go," he said pertinently.  "I have to kill
time some way.  Let's stop in here and have some ice cream," and he
swung them into a stylish eating place before they could object.
     After they were seated and the order given he turned to Parepa and
said, "Well, Peaches and Cream, how did the candy store treat you today?"
     "If you are speaking to me, I'm Parepa-Rosa and not sliced fruit.
The candy store was great.  I like it better every day."
     Frances gave a jolly laugh and reached over and pinched Parepa's
arm.  She slapped at him just as she would have big brother at home.
     Aunt Kate watched this proceeding and wondered if it wasn't just
as well that Frances's stay in the City was limited.  "They are getting
acquainted so fast," she thought, "but after all young people must know
and have young associates and Frances's fun seems only natural for
one sixteen."
     "Say folks," Frances was saying, "If it rains tomorrow night we'll
go to a show at the Grand Opera house after dinner.  I've ordered the
dinner and the carriage and will be on time so don't forget."
     "All right", said Aunt Kate.
     After Frances left them on the porch Aunt Kate suggested that
they sleep together in Parepa's bed.  "It won't be so lonesome she
said.
