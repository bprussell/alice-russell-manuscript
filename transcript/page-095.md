Soon they were surging up and down on the waves as happy as two little
children - which they were.
     "I've got a wonderful yacht at home.  Bet you can't guess what
name is on it," he laughed.
     "Nope," she said, "what is it."
     "Alice Parepa-Roza," he giggled. "Everyone wants to know what it
means," he said.
     "Tell me all about  it, Frances, and all about all the nice things
you have at home.  <u>That's</u> what <u>I</u> like to hear about," and she patted his
hand so loveingly he forgot his worries and launched on the subject she
had mentioned.
     "Really, darling, I guess I never have told you much about my life
at home.  In the first place I have so many places to call home I hardly
know which is really it.  None of them are equal to your home though,
Parepa.  Yours is ideal - mine is cold and  -  - empty.  Folks call
it beautiful - It is - to look at."
     "Oh, go on, Frances, tell me all about it!" she cuddled closer to
him and turned her face to his for a kiss.
     "I know what you want to hear about first - the garden," he said.
     "Oh, yes, the garden!  Tell me all about it.  I didn't know you
had one."
