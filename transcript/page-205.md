                              CHAPTER XII

     "Well, why don't you and your wife take a trip out there for a
few months anyway.  You'll know more about how you'll likeit by that⟦pencil-delete⟧
that time," the Doctor answered.  "You really should go soon."
     When Johnie broke this news to Allie she did not discourage it.
Instead she sat about plamning a way in which the children's welfare
would be looked after during their absence.  This settled satisfactorily
she took the matter of funds into her  onw hands.  Obtaining a loan of
five hundred dollars from the first person she approached on the subject.
     It was the fifteenth of July when they boarded the trainbround for
Denver, Colorado.  Allie had but on thing in mind.  Her good husbands
health.  They must find a health giving location.  They both enjoyed the
ride through the country immensely.  They were real chumming ⟦pencil: chummy⟧.  This
was because each was determined the other should not have time to get
homesick for the children.
     They practiced the strictest economy in traveling, limiting their
appetites to the necessities and ignoring the otherwise tempting dishes
offered in the dining car.  Each seemed to have promised themselves
that they would make the other's trip the happiest event of their lives
and each succeeded.
     They arrived in Denver about nine-thirty p.m. and were taken at
once to a gomfortable hotel.  They had several families of relatives in
this City but preferred to rest up a day or so before making their
presence known to them.
