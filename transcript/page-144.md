        "Has she  --  has she changed her mind?" and the elder man bit his
lips in anger for having asked that question so abruptly.
        "No," he said thoughtfully.  "She hasn't  --  but I have."
        "Come, come, my boy, don't feel that you are not capable of making
her happy.  You know she could be the happiest woman in the world with
you.  I can't imagine a happier union of sympathetic souls," and Mr.
Wheeler patted the miserable young fellow tenderly on the shoulder.
"Better think it over again," and he left him to retire to a restless
bed.
