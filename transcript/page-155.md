                                                                    153

Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

I see what's in it tomorrow?" she asked.  "I really want to know."
     "Yes, my darling, you can.  In the meantime I want you to prepare
to be true to yourself  -  to him and to me after hearing my secret.
Will you promise?"    Those words were familiar.  Now she was sure he
had heard.
     Her face turned to his and a long lingering kiss was her answer.
They sat there a long time as if each was afraid the sweet spell⟦strikeover: first "l" struck over another character⟧ would
be broken.
     They heard the clock in the old court house strike eleven.  "Must
go now," said Parepa and as they walked hand in hand she asked, "You
don't mind if I ask you to leave me alone when we leave Hayward Street
do you Frances?  I want to go home alone?"
     "Familiar words," laughed Frances.  "However your wish is granted,"
and once more kissing her goodbye he said, "You are a funny girl,
Parepa."
     Parepa found Uncle Will waiting up for her.  He assured her of his
anxiety for her being out alone so late but she assured him she longed
to be alone with herself at times.  Told him how when she was a little
girl at home she grew tired of having others around her and would creep
away from the house and go way down in the pasture and sit on the
hill and tell dream stories⟦strikeover: "i" overtyped on another letter⟧ to herself.  "I felt just that way tonight,"
she added but didn't tell him her game of solitary had been broken
into twice that evening.
     "Have you decided what you are going to do?" he asked her with
deep interest in his voice.
     "Not quite, Uncle Will.  I have several ideas but haven't exactly
decided."
