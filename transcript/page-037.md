                                                                    35
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     It was five-thirty now and it was planned to eat at six, rest
until seven-thirty, then practice until eight-thirty, to bed at nine,
up at six, breakfast at six-thirty, practice until seven-thirty, be
at the shop by eight.
     "That's a mighty well laid out program," said Uncle Will"and
should be carried out to the letter."
     "We make an exception of Friday evening, we take some sort of
recreation then.  George is always here on Fridays so we chose that
day for a good time." said Aunt Kate.
     "Say, don't put up a lunch for her Kate, I'll see she gets a lunch
with me.  She really should go out at noon to get a little air so I
told her she and I would get together.  I'll pay for the meals."
     "Guess I'll go now, seeing George isn't home," and Parepa watched
the stoutly built handsome Uncle disappear around the corner.
     "You are all so good to me," she said, "and I'm going to be just
as good to you".
     "Of course you are, but don't you think we should get a letter off
to your folks tonight?"
     Parepa's face clouded a little as she thought of the possibility of
her folks objecting to her staying.  They went into the house to get
supper ready.  Parepa was quiet.  Auntie suspected the reason.  Never-⟦hyphen⟧
the-less this letter had to be written and it was.  Uncle George wrote
one and Parepa wrote one.  They were placed together in an envelope and
Uncle George mailed it.
     "I'll be out there again to stay all night about Thursday and
this will give them time to make up their minds before I get there",
he said.
     "And won't I have to go with you?" asked Parepa.
