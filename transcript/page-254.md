                                                                    90
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

by a porter.  A kindly old lady came forward and was introduced to
Parepa.  She was the housekeeper.  "I'll show you to your suite," she
said and  Parepa followed her.  When she reached the top of the stair
she was met by a french girl who was to be Parepa's maid.
     "I'll draw your bath and send your clothes down to be pressed,"
she said sweetly as Parepa passed through her most embarrassing moment.
     "I'm afraid I haven't but two dresses to be pressed," she confessed.
"You see I came on a moment's notice and threw what few things I could
grab into my bag in a hurry."
     "Oh," she said and taking the travel soiled dress which Parepa
drew from the bag left the room.  She soon returned and drawing aside
the curtains that separate the bath from her dressing room motioned
Parepa to the tub and gave her the most enjoyable bath she ever had.
It was scientifically done.  After that was over she massaged her
thoroughly from her feet to her very scalp.  Her hands were manicured,
her hair beautifully dressed, her body perfumed and powdered, and after
and hour and a half of renovating she was ready to be presented to her
first love.
     Daddy Wheeler waited at the foot of the stairs for her.  He took
her by the arm and led her into a large living room at the far end of
which reclining on a luxurious lounge lay Frances.
