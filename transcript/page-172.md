school room soon took on a "pretty" look.  The Superintendent commented
favorably on it, saying he wished all his teachers were as artistic.

     She was determined to save every cent she could that she might
attend Normal School that summer one way or another.

     Most of the young swains had over stepped their bounds and had been

house.  Allie never could figure out why it should have been he who
was to fall prey to her "Endearing Young Charms".
