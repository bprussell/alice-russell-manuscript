     One boy followed another until the three were in bed.  Mr. Rice
and Mr. Brown were unable to stay at the Bradford home during this seige
so decided to return to their homes in Boston.  This worried Allie
greatly as she was concerned about the Doctor bills, etc. that had
accumulated the last few months.  She knew full well it would be some
time before they would get boarders like that again.
