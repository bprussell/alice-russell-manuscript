     Robert had just completed his first year in the Princeville High
School.  In fact the very day school closed his father took desperately
sick while riding a plow in the field.  He was rushed at once to a
Doctor who realizing the seriousness of his condition hurried him to
the Peoria Methodist hospital.  It was decided best after three weeks
observation to take him to Mayo Brothers Clinic at Rochester, Minnesota.
     He was there nine weeks.  After a most serious experimental operation
his strength came back to him slowly.  After his return home it was
necessary for his physician to care for the wound and prescribe for him
daily.  It was over a year before his services were considered unnecessary
The expense of this sickness ran well into the thousands.  It plunged
them into debt deeper than ever.  There seemed no use to try to keep on.
Yet could they give up? - No, not they.
     These people knew no such word as fail and so gritty was the whole
family that they seemed to double their efforts.  Robert, young as he
was,became the head of farming operations, Richard was not far behind,
keeping up a man's end of the work.  Milking the cows, separating the
milk, sloping and feeding the hogs, cleaning stables, and doing all
