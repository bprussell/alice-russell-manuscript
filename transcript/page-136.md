Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

Joseph - "It's awful to be poor isn't it "Pep' (his pet name for
          her.)
Parepa - "No one knows that better than I do.  How you coming with
          your college work?"
Joseph - "Slow - no money - no progress you know."
Parepa - "Don't I know though.  I'm looking for a dear friend of
          mine about tomorrow that is so rich he don't know what to
          do with it all.  He aims to do a lot of good with it
          though.  Just go about helping wherever he finds a need for
          it.  Wouldn't that be a grand life though?"
Joseph - "Yes, but I'd rather be a Doctor."
Parepa - "I'm going to tell you something Joe." Drawing the last
          line on her board and walking over to his side of the
          room.  "This fellow wants me to help him spend it."
Joseph - "Parepa,!. You don't mean to tell me this chap has proposed
          to you?"
Parepa - "I mean just that."
