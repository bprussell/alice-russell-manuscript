Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     After the lights were out she cuddled up to Parepa and told her
how much she and Uncle George loved her and how they hoped she would
always be careful of herself and not get too familiar with strange
girls and boys.  She told her of some of the pit falls she must watch
for.
     Aunt Kate realized better than this young girl ever would that her
modest charms, physical and intellectual, made a peculiar appeal to
the opposite set⟦pencil: sex⟧ as well as being the envy of her own gender.
     Since her stay with them Aunt Kate was often surprized and pleased at
the super-intelligence unfolding in her.  She knew from the quick and
precise way she mastered her music lessons there was no doubt that any
thing she might undertake would be a success.
     "You must try to satisfy yourself with your job and music for a
few years at least.  By that time you'll be experienced enough in life
to choose and judge your associates and pleasures.  You know we will
see to it that you have all the pleasures and companionship a girl
your age needs to stimulate them in the meantime."  Said Aunt Kate giving
Parepa a hug and kiss.
     "Oh!  Aunt Kate you are all so good to me.  I wouldn't do one thing
to make you sorry for anything. "
     "Good night, dear, and sweet dreams," said Aunt Kate.
     "Nightie night", echoed Parepa and they were soon in dream land.
But before Parepa slept she offered up a special prayer that Mamma and
Papa would let her stay here where she was so  happy.
     The next day was an anxious one for Parepa.  She could hardly wait
until time to go home and hear Uncle George's message from home.
     It was four-thirty now and turning her eyes from the clock they
fell directly upon Uncle George.
