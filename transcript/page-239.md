                                                                    75
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois
                         CHAPTER XX

                                        Tucson Arizona
                                        Jan. 30, 1932

     Editor, Peoria Tribune
     Peoria, Illinois
          I am endeavoring to ascertain the present wherea-⟦hyphen⟧
     bouts of Alice Parepa-Roza Wesley, who resided with relatives
     in your City, in the vicinity of the Bethany Baptist
     Church during the years 1898 to 1903.  At that time she
     studied dramatic Art with Mrs Rose Mendenhall Breen and
     Piano and voice with a Mrs Slay.
          Though only a young ⟦strikeover: she⟧ ⟦pencil-insert: girl⟧ she earned her way and paid
     for her lessons by working in a candy store on Main Street
     opposite the Court House.
          I learned many years ago that her relatives died and
     she became a school teacher in the West.
          I have tried repeatedly the last five years to locate
     her by radio, correspondence and the press.  I remembered
     the names of Arthur Youngblood and Joseph Donald, friends
     of hers.  I wrote them hoping to locate her but the letters
     were returned.  No doubt she married.  I never did.
          It is very much to her interest that she be found.  As
     she will remember I have no living relatives.  I have been
     very successfully financially.  My health has failed me.  I
     have heard my physician's warning.  It can't be long.
          I assure you any effort you may put forth to locate her
     will be greatly appreciated.
          Thanking you.
                                        I an truly yours,
                                        F.L. Emmerson

(By J.C. Wheeler)
