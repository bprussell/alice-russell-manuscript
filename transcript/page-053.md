                                                                    51
Submitted by:
Mrs Alice Reynolds Russell
Mt. Hawley Road
Edelstein, Illinois

     "Couldn't I interest them in my Junior Choir?"  We need their
voices so much."
     "I shall be leaving Peoria soon," said Frances, "'Twould hardly
be worth starting now."
     "I'd love it," said Parepa.
     So it was arranged then and there that she was to attend rehearsal
every Thursday night.
     Frances sat with them on the porch for sometime that afternoon.
Uncle George was sure he caught him looking longingly at Parepa several
times.
     "I must go now, good people," he said, "and write a letter to
Daddy Wheeler.  By the way did I tell you were leave most anyday now?"
     "Then what will I do for someone to play with?" said Parepa.
     "Oh, you will get acquainted fast now that you are going to sing
in the Junior Choir," he returned.  "I wish I were going to be here."
     "We wish you were too," said Aunt Kate, "we'll all miss you so
much.  But, of course, you'll write to us."
     "Don't forget us when you come back to America," added Uncle
George.
     "I'll never forget you - and Parepa,"taking both hands into his
his "You'll write to me real often won't you?"
     "Surely" she said and blushed a pretty pink as he squeezed her
hands.
     "Good night", and he swung himself across the street.
