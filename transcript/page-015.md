     "All right.  Then I think I hear Uncle George talking to Frances

     Parepa-Roza tried so hard to help but her fingers were all thumbs.
Finally all was ready and Parepa-Roza was not too fussed to appreciate
the beauty of Aunt Kate's well appointed table.  The china, silver,
crystal, and flowers combined to make it a perfect picture.  She
heard them coming in now and what would she do?  There was no pantry
door to hide behind in Aunt Kate's house, as she was used to doing
at home when she wanted to be unnoticed and especially un-introduced
to strangers.
     "This is our neice, Alice Parepa-Roza, Frances.  She is going
to visit us a while and I hope you two will have lots of good times
together."
     "I'm sure we will", said Frances and Parepa-Roza smiled back at
him without half trying.  "Do you play tennis?"
     "No, I don't, but I'd love to learn", and she wandered how she
happened to make such a long sentence to a stranger that she had never
seen before.
     "What say we all go over after supper and teach her a bit about
the game, wifie?" spoke up Uncle George.  Then on second thought he
added, "I expect she is tired ⟦strikeover: too⟧ though,after that long jaunt in
my old tea wagon."
     Parepa-Roza could have squeezed him for that, seemed as if he
always said the right thing at the right time.
