     Parepa slowly ascended the stairs and on entering her living room
saw on the table a letter from home.  Its sides fairly bulged with its
messages.  Her maid stood waiting.  "I'll rest an hour then dress for
luncheon," she told her and taking her letter from its envelope she
laughed as she recognized the handwriting of four of her kingdom.  Each
had added their bit to assure her she need not worry about them.  She
kissed each one then slipping into her kimona she relaxed on the
lounge.

     The doings of the forenoon had no room in the thoughts of this woman.
Tears crept into her eyes as she read over and over each of her families'
messages.  She was homesick.  Yes, there was no doubt about it.  She
gave way to a torrent of tears.  Try as she would she could not control
them.  Her maid asked timidly, "Are you sick,⟦strikeover: comma struck over a stray letter⟧ Mrs Bradford?"
