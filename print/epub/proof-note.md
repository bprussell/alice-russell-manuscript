# About This Proof {.unnumbered .unlisted}

::: proof-note
This is a reader's proof of the edited text — a chance to read the whole
book straight through before anything is typeset for print. It is not
the finished edition: the foreword, title pages and facsimile plates of
the original manuscript come later.

The text has been lightly edited from the typescript: obvious typing
slips corrected, her own pencil corrections applied, and spelling
modernized. Her words, grammar and phrasing are her own. Every change is
logged against the original page transcriptions, so anything can be
put back.

**Noting changes:** for anything that catches your eye, jot down the
chapter and a few words of the sentence (for example, *Book I, Ch. XVI —
"trusted her with evening entertainments"*). That is enough to find it.
:::
