Sealed Secrets follows a dreamy, freckle-faced farm girl, Alice Parepa-Roza Wesley, from her father’s farm to her aunt and uncle’s home in Peoria. Kashema returns to her years later.

Written by Alice Reynolds Russell of Edelstein, Illinois, early in the twentieth century, kept for a hundred years in a box of loose typewritten pages, and set in type for the first time for her family.

Alice Parapa-Rosa Reynolds Russell (1881–1975) taught at the Kellogg school, raised three sons on farms around Edelstein, and lectured across central Illinois on her flowers and her button collection. She wrote five books; these two are the only ones known to survive.
