# Cover brief

For designing the cover (e.g. in Claude Design). Printer: Lulu, hardcover,
6 × 9 in, black-and-white interior on cream paper, about 285 pages. The
cover itself may be full color: it is priced separately from the interior.

## Exact size: get it from Lulu

The spine width depends on the final page count and paper, so take the
dimensions from Lulu's own template rather than estimating:

1. Start a print book on lulu.com: Hardcover, 6 × 9 in (US Trade),
   Standard black & white, 60# cream paper, and either **Casewrap** or
   **Linen wrap with dust jacket**.
2. Upload `print/book/interior.pdf`.
3. Download the cover template Lulu generates. It gives the full spread
   size, spine width, bleed, safe area and (for a jacket) flap widths.
   Design to those numbers and export a single-page PDF.

Layout of the spread, left to right: (back flap), back cover, spine,
front cover, (front flap). Keep all text inside the safe area; let
background color and the image run into the bleed.

## Look

- **Image:** a locket. In the story, a locket holds the secrets of the
  first book; an old-fashioned oval or heart-shaped gold locket, perhaps
  slightly open or on a fine chain, fits the period (about 1890–1930).
  Engraved, photographic, or painterly all suit; avoid anything that
  looks modern or glossy.
- **Type:** Sorts Mill Goudy (the interior face; free, OFL, in
  `print/fonts/`), using its italic for the titles, as on the title page.
- **Mood:** an heirloom novel; muted, warm colors (deep green, oxblood,
  navy, or cream and sepia), restrained ornament.

> SEALED SECRETS & KASHEMA · ALICE REYNOLDS RUSSELL

**Back cover** (or front flap, if there is a jacket); spoiler-free:

> *Sealed Secrets* follows a dreamy, freckle-faced farm girl, Alice
> Parepa-Roza Wesley, from her father's farm to her aunt and uncle's home
> in Peoria. *Kashema* returns to her years later.
>
> Written by Alice Reynolds Russell of Edelstein, Illinois, early in the
> twentieth century, kept for a hundred years in a box of loose
> typewritten pages, and set in type for the first time for her family.

**Back flap, or lower back cover** (optional): the frontispiece photo
(`print/book/images/alice-and-john.jpg`) or the 50th-anniversary photo
(`docs/allie-and-johnie.heic`), with:

> Alice Parepa-Rosa Reynolds Russell (1881–1975) taught at the Kellogg
> school, raised three sons on farms around Edelstein, and lectured across central Illinois on her flowers and her button
> collection. She wrote five books; these two are the only ones known
> to survive.

No barcode or ISBN is needed for a private printing.
