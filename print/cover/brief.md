# Cover brief

For designing the dust jacket (e.g. in Claude Design). Printer: Lulu,
**hardcover, linen wrap with dust jacket**, 6 × 9 in, black-and-white
interior on cream paper, 286 pages. The jacket may be full color: it is
priced separately from the interior.

The book itself is plain linen boards; the jacket carries the design.

## Exact size: get it from Lulu

The spine width depends on the final page count and paper, so take the
dimensions from Lulu's own template rather than estimating:

1. Start a print book on lulu.com: Hardcover, 6 × 9 in (US Trade),
   Standard black & white, 60# cream paper, **Linen wrap with dust
   jacket**.
2. Upload `print/book/interior.pdf`.
3. Download the dust-jacket template Lulu generates. It gives the full
   spread size, spine width, flap widths, bleed and safe area. Design to
   those numbers and export a single-page PDF.
4. Choose the linen color and the spine foil (see below).

Layout of the jacket, left to right: **back flap, back cover, spine,
front cover, front flap**. The flaps fold inside the boards. Keep all text
inside the safe area and clear of the folds; let the background color run
into the bleed.

## Lulu's numbers (from the project, 286 pp, 60# cream, linen wrap)

- Full jacket: **20.688 × 9.75 in** (525.48 × 247.65 mm), one-page PDF,
  fonts embedded, layers flattened, under 100 MB.
- Spine: **0.938 in**. Flaps: **3.25 in** each. Flap fold: **0.25 in**
  each.
- So, left to right: back flap 3.25 · fold 0.25 · back cover 6.375 ·
  spine 0.938 · front cover 6.375 · fold 0.25 · front flap 3.25
  (= 20.688 in). Check bleed and safe lines against Lulu's downloaded
  template before export.
- Linen: green, gold foil. Spine stamping (42 characters combined):
  title `SEALED SECRETS & KASHEMA`, author `RUSSELL`.

## Look

- **Image:** a locket (as designed: the engraved gold locket on a chain).
  In the story, a locket holds the secrets of the first book.
- **Type:** Sorts Mill Goudy (the interior face; free, OFL, in
  `print/fonts/`), italic for the titles, as on the title page.
- **Colors:** deep green ground with antique gold (as designed); oxblood,
  navy or sepia are the alternatives.
- **Linen boards:** choose a linen color that goes with the jacket
  (green, or black or tan), with **gold** foil on the spine. Suggested
  spine stamping, which Lulu limits to a short line:
  `SEALED SECRETS & KASHEMA`.

## Text

**Front cover** (as designed)

> *Sealed Secrets*
> & *Kashema*
>
> ALICE REYNOLDS RUSSELL

**Spine** (reads top to bottom; as designed)

> SEALED SECRETS & KASHEMA · ALICE REYNOLDS RUSSELL

**Front flap**: the spoiler-free description.

> *Sealed Secrets* follows a dreamy, freckle-faced farm girl, Alice
> Parepa-Roza Wesley, from her father's farm to her aunt and uncle's home
> in Peoria. *Kashema* returns to her years later.
>
> Written by Alice Reynolds Russell of Edelstein, Illinois, early in the
> twentieth century, kept for a hundred years in a box of loose
> typewritten pages, and set in type for the first time for her family.

**Back flap**: about the author, with the 50th-anniversary photo
(`print/cover/allie-and-johnie-50th.jpg`, cleaned up from the family
history), since the wedding photo is already the frontispiece inside.

> Alice Parepa-Rosa Reynolds Russell (1881–1975) taught at the Kellogg
> school, raised three sons on farms around Edelstein, and lectured
> across central Illinois on her flowers and her button collection. She
> wrote five books; these two are the only ones known to survive.
>
> *Photograph: Alice and John Russell on their fiftieth wedding
> anniversary, 1955.*

**Back cover**: with the description and bio on the flaps, the back can
be quiet. Suggested: the green ground with a small gold ornament (the
diamond rule from the front) and one line from the family history,
centered:

> “An autobiography that glides freely between the factual and the
> fanciful. You are never just sure which is which.”
>
> — from the Russell family history
